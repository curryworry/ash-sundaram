DV360 YouTube Channel Analyzer

Automated COPPA Compliance & Brand Safety Analysis
by Acquire NZ

Purpose: This automation tool analyzes YouTube channel placements from Display & Video 360 (DV360) advertising reports to identify children's content and ensure COPPA compliance for advertising campaigns.

Overview

The DV360 Channel Analyzer automatically processes YouTube placement reports to help advertisers maintain compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and ensure brand safety across their advertising campaigns.

Key Features

  • Automated Email Processing: Monitors Gmail for DV360 placement reports sent as email attachments
  • Children's Content Detection: Identifies channels targeting children to ensure COPPA compliance
  • Brand Safety Analysis: Assesses content verticals, brand suitability, and safety scores
  • Intelligent Caching: Stores analysis results to avoid re-analyzing known channels
  • List Generation: Creates inclusion/exclusion lists for DV360 campaign targeting
  • Automated Reporting: Sends analysis results via email with downloadable CSV files

How It Works

  1. Email Monitoring: The system searches Gmail for DV360 placement reports (CSV files sent as zip attachments)
  2. Data Extraction: Downloads and extracts YouTube channel URLs from the report
  3. Pre-filtering: Uses keyword matching to identify obvious children's content (e.g., "nursery rhymes", "baby songs")
  4. Cache Check: Queries database for previously analyzed channels to avoid redundant API calls
  5. Metadata Fetching: Retrieves channel information from YouTube Data API (subscribers, video titles, descriptions)
  6. AI Analysis: Uses OpenAI GPT-4 to categorize content and assess:
    • Children's content classification (COPPA compliance)
    • Content vertical and sub-niche
    • Brand safety score
    • Purchase intent signals
    • Geographic focus and language
  7. Results Storage: Saves categorization results to Firestore for future reference
  8. List Generation: Creates two CSV files:
    • Inclusion List: Safe channels approved for advertising
    • Exclusion List: Children's content channels to block
  9. Report Delivery: Emails summary statistics and download links to stakeholders

Gmail API Usage & Data Privacy

What Gmail Permissions We Request:

  • gmail.readonly: Read access to search for and download DV360 placement reports sent as email attachments
  • gmail.send: Send access to deliver analysis results and CSV download links via email

How We Use Your Gmail Data:

  • The application only accesses emails containing DV360 placement reports (identified by subject line: "YouTube Placement Check - DV360")
  • Email attachments (CSV files) are downloaded temporarily for processing and deleted after analysis
  • No email content is stored permanently - only the extracted YouTube channel URLs are cached
  • Analysis results are sent to authorized recipients via email
  • The application only accesses the authenticated user's own Gmail account
  • No data is shared with third parties

Data Retention:

  • Email attachments: Processed and immediately deleted (within 60 minutes)
  • YouTube channel analysis results: Stored indefinitely in Firestore for caching purposes
  • Email messages: Not stored - only accessed temporarily for attachment download

User Control:

  • Users can revoke Gmail access at any time via Google Account Permissions
  • Revoking access will stop the automation but will not delete cached analysis results
